# Tabrador Retriever privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Tabrador Retriever is a Chrome extension. When a link would open a page that is already open in another tab, it switches to that tab instead of opening a duplicate.

## What it reads

The web addresses (URLs) of your open tabs, and of links as they open in a new tab. It compares them to find a tab that already shows the same page. It can't read the content of the pages you visit.

## What it stores

One setting: whether you have paused the extension with its toolbar button. It's kept in Chrome's extension storage on your device.

## What it sends

Nothing. No data leaves your browser. There are no servers, no analytics, no tracking and no third parties.

## Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the new version is published.
